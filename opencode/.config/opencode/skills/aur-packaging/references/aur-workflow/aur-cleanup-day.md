# AUR Cleanup Day

Source: `/usr/share/doc/arch-wiki-zh-cn/html/zh-cn/AUR_扫除日.html`

## Purpose

Community criteria for removal, retention, or orphaning of obsolete AUR packages.

## Operational guidance

- Support cleanup requests with concrete English evidence: broken sources, deprecation, replacement, official inclusion, persistent incompatibility, or abandonment.
- Prefer retention or orphaning when a package remains useful, repairable, or required by another package.
- Cleanup nominations do not delete packages automatically; Package Maintainers make the final decision.
- Use normal AUR requests for deletion, merge, or orphan actions rather than treating cleanup discussion as an automated command.
